Delete your account

Your account can be deleted completely, with everything in it. There are two ways: one inside the app and one without it.

In the app — immediately

The fastest route, and the only one that takes effect at once:

  1. Tap your profile picture in the top right
  2. Choose Profile
  3. At the very bottom: Delete my account
  4. Confirm the prompt

Deletion happens immediately. You are signed out afterwards and can no longer sign in with that address.

Without the app — by email

No device at hand, or the app already uninstalled? Then send us a short message:

To: info@juicyslaedle.de
Subject: Delete account

Please send it from the email address the account was created with. That is our only way to verify the request really comes from you — otherwise anyone could have someone else's account deleted.

We delete within 30 days and confirm by reply. In practice it is much faster.

What gets deleted

There is no undo. The data is removed rather than merely hidden — we cannot bring it back either. If you want to keep your recipes, save them beforehand.

A running subscription does not end automatically

The subscription runs through Google Play or the App Store and must be cancelled there separately. We can neither end nor refund it — see Terms of Use § 5. So cancel in the store first, then delete the account.

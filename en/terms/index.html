§ 2 What the app does

Reel Gusto fetches the publicly available post behind a link you provide, turns its text into a recipe and stores that recipe in your account. Alongside it come a weekly plan, favourites, categories, a cook mode and the option to share your cookbook with other accounts.

Processing is automated. There is no entitlement to any particular result — in particular not to every post containing usable ingredients or steps.

An account is personal. You may not pass it on, nor access our interfaces by automated means.

Every new account receives a number of free imports (currently 15). They do not expire. After that, a subscription is required for new recipes.

Recipes already saved remain accessible permanently, with or without a subscription and after any cancellation: reading, cooking, the weekly plan and favourites continue to work. Only the creation of new recipes is blocked.
